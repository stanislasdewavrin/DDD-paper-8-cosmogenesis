==========================================================================
SUMMARY
==========================================================================

The DESI DR2 results (March 2025) provide statistical preference at
the 2.8 to 4.2 sigma level (depending on the supernova sample combined
with BAO) that the dark-energy equation of state evolves with redshift,
in a very specific way: w_0 > -1 today, w_a < 0, with a smooth
crossing of the phantom barrier (w = -1) near z ~ 0.5. This
combination is incompatible with a cosmological constant and
challenging for single-field quintessence, which explains the recent
surge of theoretical proposals (quintom, non-minimal couplings,
dark-sector interactions, modified gravity).

In this manuscript I present a discrete-lattice cosmogenesis model
that reproduces the three distinctive features of DESI DR2
simultaneously, without introducing any new field, coupling, or
modification of general relativity. The mechanism is simply: a
lattice initialised with R_i = 0 on every node, filled progressively
from a single localised exponentially-growing founding impulse,
under the conservative drainage rule of Paper I of the same series.
Numerical integration of the dynamics yields:

  - m_inf = -3.72 +/- 0.04   (DESI: -3.72; ~0.5 sigma agreement)
  - m_0   = +1.59 +/- 0.18   (DESI: +1.65; ~0.3 sigma agreement)
  - phantom-crossing redshift z* ~ 0.45 (DESI: ~0.45)

across an ensemble of 10 independent lattice realisations. Both
extracted parameters are statistically indistinguishable from the
DESI DR2 values within the realisation-to-realisation scatter. The
phantom phase emerges naturally as the filling phase of the
cosmogenesis cascade; the phantom crossing is the moment when the
filling cascade saturates the lattice; the today quintessence regime
is the post-saturation dilution.

==========================================================================
WHY THIS MATTERS NOW
==========================================================================

1. The DESI DR2 signal is the most exciting cosmological observation
   of the past decade. Multiple competing theoretical proposals are
   being put forward, each adding new physical ingredients (extra
   fields, couplings, modified actions). The present model differs
   in being parameter-economical: the same lattice rule that has
   previously been shown in the DDD series to reproduce emergent
   gravity, photon deflection, and the fine-structure constant also
   accounts for the late-time dark-energy phenomenology.

2. The phantom-crossing behaviour is forced by the structure of the
   model (transition between filling and dilution regimes), not
   fine-tuned. This is qualitatively different from minimally-
   coupled scalar fields where crossing requires extra degrees of
   freedom.

3. The model makes distinguishing predictions that future surveys
   (DESI DR3, Euclid, Vera Rubin Observatory, Roman Space Telescope)
   can test: distinct asymptotic future, large-scale matter pattern
   inherited from the founding impulse, possible reading of the
   Hubble tension as a residual incompleteness of the cosmogenesis
   cascade, and (developed in the companion Paper IX) a small but
   computable departure from the Etherington reciprocity relation
   d_L = (1+z)^2 d_A.

==========================================================================
NOVELTY AND PRIOR ART
==========================================================================

The Discrete Drainage Dynamics (DDD) framework on which this work
builds is documented in companion manuscripts I-VII of the same
author. These manuscripts establish the conservative drainage rule
on a random geometric graph (Paper I), the recovery of weak-field
gravity (Paper II), the kinematic clock-rate suppression and Weyl
spinor structure (Paper III), photon deflection and Shapiro delay
(Paper IV), falsification tests (Paper V), the emergent gauge
sector and the fine-structure constant (Paper VI), and the
gravity-gauge cross-coupling (Paper VII). The present (Paper VIII)
is the cosmological application.

The novel contributions of the present paper are:

  - The cosmogenetic regime: the lattice is initialised empty
    (R_i = 0) rather than at a uniform reference value.
  - Connection of the phantom phase observed by DESI to lattice
    filling dynamics.
  - Quantitative comparison with DESI DR2 across an ensemble of
    independent realisations and across a factor of 4 in lattice
    size.
  - Sensitivity and null controls (variants in source profile,
    pumping rate, no-shutoff variant) confirming that the m_eff
    signature is robust.
